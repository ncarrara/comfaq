## Question

With the new GW FAQ about taking wheeling into account for allowing charges, can you declare charges with Swiftstride if the opponent is within M+9 but with wheel M+12?

## Ruling

The opponent unit has to be both within M+9 maximum charge range and it has to be possible to conduct the charge with M+9 including the wheels.
