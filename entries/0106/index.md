## Question

Does the controlling player choose the direction before or after rolling the distance for a spell, effect, or model (such as a fanatic) that moves in a random distance?

## Ruling

Choose before rolling. RAW is ambiguous, and the functional difference is minimal. This is less a "correct" answer and more of having something in place to keep the game flowing.
