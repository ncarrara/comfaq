## Question

Spectral Doppelganger - Which weapon can a model use for the spell? When are the hits of a Strike Last Weapon resolved? What happens if the caster dies before the attacks are resolved after he made the cast?

## Ruling

Any melee weapon the model carries can be used, magical or not. For a Strike Last weapon, the attacks are resolved at Initiative 1, unmodified as the spell gives you a initiative value. If the caster dies before the attacks are made, the attacks will be resolved at I1.
