## Question

Can models in the rear ranks of a formed unit shoot if they are able to draw a Line of Sight? What does "normally" mean in the sentence “Normally, only models in the front rank of a unit can shoot” within [Check Line of Sight]?

## Ruling

No. "Normally" means “as a rule”, which applies unless another rule explicitly overrides it (Volley Fire, Vantage Point)
