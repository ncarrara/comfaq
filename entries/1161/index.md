## Question

If a character is within one fighting rank they cannot move through the ranks to join another. Might this prevent them from accepting a challenge?

## Ruling

No. If a character is eligible to accept a challenge they can do so wherever they are in a unit and will move through the ranks into base contact with their opponent. However, if it is awkward to move a model, it is quite acceptable for the players to agree it has moved and fight the challenge as if the combatants were in base contact, thus avoiding too much disruption.
