## Question

Q: The 1.5 errata states that "Any model that is within a fighting rank and within a number of inches equal to its Movement of the enemy unit can fight." The Random Movement special rule states that "Models with this special rule do not have a normal Movement characteristic. Instead, a dice roll is given (2D6, for example). When a model with this special rule moves, roll the dice to determine its maximum movement." How should this interaction be played?

## Ruling

A) Because the unit does not have a normal movement characteristic when not actively moving, treat the movement value during combat as M (-). This means that models with Random Movement can only make attacks when in base contact with an enemy model.
B) Those units still have a Movement characteristic that deviates from the norm. When a unit with Random Movement has models in a fighting rank but not in base-to-base contact with an enemy models, roll their Random Movement dice when their combat is chosen during step 1.1 of the Combat phase. Use the number rolled as the unit's Movement for this combat phase as the unit's Movement characteristic when determining which models outside of base-to-base can attack. If the unit with Random Movement fights in a 2nd combat during the same combat phase, when that new combat is chosen the unit with Random Movement will roll again to determine this range.
