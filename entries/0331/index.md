## Question

When can you declare a counter charge? Can you use Counter Charge if you are not able to conduct the pivot or reach the opponent?

## Ruling

Counter Charge is a charge reaction which does not underly the requirements for declaring a charge. All limits of declaring Counter Charge are specificed in GW FAQ. You can declare a counter charge outside of line of sight and without having the ability to reach your opponent. If you hit an opponent unit with the counter charge move after the pivot, you count as doing accidental contact. If you cannot fully pivot, you will not move at all and if you cannot move after the pivot, you will also not move, but you still count as charging.
