## Question

When two units are in combat with the same facing of an enemy unit that flees from combat, how is the pursuit resolved? Do the units block each other when moving toward or pivoting to face the fleeing enemy?

## Ruling

Each pursuit happens sequentially, with the controlling player choosing their order. Pivot moves always ignore the presence of other units and terrain. When the first unit makes its pursuit, it may end overlapping with another friendly unit that was part of that combat (even though this is normally now allowed); and may move through those units until the first units move is complete. The other units do ignore the other units when making its own pivot aswell, but their move will often be blocked by the unit that has moved first. If the unit can only partly pivot and will overlap after pivot with the unit that has moved first, it pivots instead as far as it is able toward the fleeing enemy and does not move further.
