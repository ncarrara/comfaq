## Question

A unit cannot Stand & Shoot if the distance between it and the charging unit is less than the Movement characteristic of the charging unit. But if a unit is charged by two (or more) enemy units and only one is too close, can it Stand & Shoot at the other?

## Ruling

No. If any of the charging units are too close to the charged unit for it to make a Stand & Shoot charge reaction, it cannot Stand & Shoot.
