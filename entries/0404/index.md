## Question

How/When can units draw Line of Sight to points on Hills or in Forests, for example when targeting a point with Cannon Fire?

## Ruling

The GW FAQ states that Line of Sight is only obstructed to points behind such terrain features and not in these Terrain Features. For Vantage Points, treat any target point as a unit. Any point on a hill can be seen over models and units, provided they are no closer to the center of the hill as the potential target point.
