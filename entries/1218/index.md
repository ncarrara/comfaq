## Question

A pursuing unit must move directly towards the fleeing enemy, but must it move forwards whilst doing so, or can it move sideways?

## Ruling

As stated on page 123, units must move forwards unless performing a manoeuvre (or when Giving Ground). This includes whilst pursuing.
