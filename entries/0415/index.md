## Question

What is Shooting or a Shooting Attack?
Can i use special rules that grant abilites to be used in the Shooting Phase after Marching or Charging?
What about evasive or lone character protection against such abilities?

## Ruling

Besides the attacks with Missile weapons units do when selected in the shooting phase for shooting, the following abilities are considered to be shooting or shooting attacks:
- Abilities that inflict hits, wounds suffered or wounds lost against a target are shooting attacks
- Magic Missiles and Vortexes are defined as shooting, if they have a target or not
- Any ability that is introduced as "shootizrzarzrng " or "instead of shooting normally" or an "attack in the shooting phase" or being "used in the shooting phase" (Column of Fire, Pidgeon Bomb, Wailing Dirge, Breath Weapon  etc.) is shooting

Anything else happening in the Shooting Phase is not a shooting attack (Zzzap! Ability of the Doomwheel)

You cannot use a Shooting Attack or Shooting after you marched (including abilities that can be used in combat like Wailing Dirge or Storm Call)

Evasive and Lone Character Protection can be used against:
- Any of those abilities affirmed to be shooting/attacks above thon a models base.

Evasive and Lone Character Protection cannot be used against any of those abilites that have no target (Burning Gaze, Gaze of Gork, Strangleroots, Cannon Fire if the target point was not on the models base).
