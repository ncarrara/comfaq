## Question

A skirmisher unit that has formed up into Close Formation during combat must maintain this formation until it is no longer engaged at the end of the Combat phase. How does reform work while the unit is in this Close Formation (FBIGO, restrain & reform, Catching the Curs)? How does being formed up interact with Fleeing and Panic? Can you still use Chariot Runners when being Formed up?

## Ruling

Close Formation” is read as a distinct mode within Skirmish Formation: the unit is still a skirmisher, but currently “formed up” (edge‑to‑edge, ranked). While formed up, the unit behaves like a formed unit for movement purposes: pivoting and rearranging ranks/files are allowed, and it may adopt another keyword formation (e.g., Open Order) if permitted. The unit will also flee exactly like a formed unit. However, it must remain formed up until the end of the Combat phase, and does not re‑form up again when pursuing into a new enemy (it keeps the ranks/files it already has).

For Panic, the rules for Skirmishers & Panic speak of being Skirmish Formation and not of Ranks, which the formed up skirmisher formation unit still fulfills. Therefore, it will only cause panic in Skirmisher units if fleeing through and if another skirmisher unit flees through them while being formed up, they will still suffer panic.

You cannot use Chariot Runners to move with a Chariot through formed up Skirmishers.
