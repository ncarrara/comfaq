## Question

Since Killing Blow / Monster Slayer only states "roll a 6 on a wound roll" and Ethereal states "cannot be wounded" - does Killing Blow / Monster Slayer with non-magical attacks actually work against Ethereal?

## Ruling

No! Killing Blow / Monster Slayer states that there has to be an "unsaved wound" for it to work - but Ethereal units can only be wounded by magical attacks.
