## Question

A unit affected by certain spells (Earthen Ramparts, Miasmic Mirage), formation (marching column) or that has rallied cannot charge or make a charge moves. How does this interact with Random Movers contacting enemies, Counter Charge or Pursuit Into a Fresh Enemy?

## Ruling

Since all these count as charging, and therefore as charge  moves for special rules like Frenzy or Furious Charge and Impact Hits, you cannot conduct these  when  under such effects.
