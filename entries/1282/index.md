## Question

If a Stubborn character joins a unit that is not Stubborn and that unit loses its first combat of the game, does the character remain Stubborn until they have an opportunity to make use of the rule later on?

## Ruling

No. Stubborn is used the first time a unit loses combat. If a character is unable to use the rule due to being part of a unit that is not Stubborn, the opportunity is lost.
