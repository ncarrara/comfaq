## Question

When do you choose the Lore for an Arcane Familiar (or Wizarding Hat)? When do you choose how many spells are rolled from each Lore if knowing more than a single Lore?

## Ruling

The chosen Lore has to be noted in the army list. When randomly generating spells, first declare how many spells you want to generate on each Lore. Roll separately, re-rolling duplicate spells for each Lore. A single randomly generated spell can be replaced with the signature spell of the same Lore, or with a spell from the army lore the wizard may have
