## Question

Are baggage trains considered impassable terrain for the purposes of the crunch rule?

## Ruling

No. Baggage trains are not impassible terrain for shooting purposes, including the Crunch rule for cannon fire. They are treated as impassible only for the purpose of moving and positioning units.
