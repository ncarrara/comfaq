## Question

How do "laser beam" spells and attacks such as Gaze of Gork, Burning Gaze and Lightning Strike (from the Warp Lightning Canon) work?

When the game rules calls for using a line (to determine models hit from "laser beams" special rules such as Gaze of Gork, or by models with the Cannon Fire special rule, or for the secondary objective Domination), what is the thickness of the line? Can it ever touch two models in a formed up unit at the same time over the same point, given that these models touch?

## Ruling

Once the spell is cast, choose a direction, then determine the distance of the line. This line does not require a target and therefore ignores all targeting rules, i.e. it can be drawn over LoS-blocking terrain, hit models in combat, lone characters, fanatics, and models the caster doesn't have LoS to.

In geometry, a straight line is an infinitely long object with no width, depth, or curvature. Although models in a formed up unit are touching edge-to-edge, they do not share a space, and since the line has no width, there is no point on the line that can be over two models simultaneously.
Practically, this means that a laser beam can only be crossing a single model at any given point, and so can never be shot exactly alongside the line where two files (or ranks) of a formed up units touch in order to hit both, or exactly over the point where the corners of 4 bases are touching in order to hit all 4.
The path of a cannonball follows the same principles, with the added restriction of only being able to hit a single model per rank under the path when firing in the unit's front arc, or per file when firing in the unit's flank.
Finally, this means that if a unit straddles over two quarters for Domination, it is not possible to position it so that all of its models are wholly within one quarter or the other.
