## Question

Skirmishers can defend a Low Linear Obstacle (LLO) by "moving as many models as possible into base contact with it." What exactly does this mean?

## Ruling

In order to defend a LLO, a skirmish unit must move as many models as can reach the LLO within a single movement, and have at least one model in the unit end its move with one side of its base fully in contact with the long edge of the LLO. When a charge is declared against the defending skirmish unit, the enemy unit will move as normal for charging a skirmish unit. If they contact the opposite long edge of the defended LLO, the unit will then wheel to align to the LLO. The defending skirmish unit will then form ranks as though there were not a gap between the two units, attempting to move as many models as possible into (not technically) B2B and forming ranks behind those models as normal.
