## Question

How do you flee along an impassable terrain? If a fleeing unit reaches impassable terrain, how does it move?

## Ruling

First, begin to flee as normal.

If the flee move would cause the unit to contact impassable terrain, it must pivot around its centre as soon as it is possible for the unit’s flank to be flush against the terrain feature, while facing the direction which will allow it to resume its flee move along its initial flee path as directly as possible. The unit will continue to flee along the side of the impassable terrain until it is able to pivot again around the feature's corner, and move along the next side of the feature (this might need to be done multiple times in case of a non-square feature, or multiple impassable features), until its centre is back on top of the initial direction of flight, at which point the unit will pivot in order to resume fleeing in its initial direction.

In the case where the unit is too close to pivot its flank against the impassable feature (this is often the case for very wide units, even if they are not backed against the feature), it must first pivot around its centre while facing the direction which will allow it to resume the unit’s flee move along its initial flee path as directly as possible. It then moves forward (taking peril tests as required), until pivoting with the unit’s flank against the feature is possible, then follow the procedure described above.
