## Question

If a character is in any rank of a unit other than the front rank (or the first fighting rank, in the case of units engaged in combat), for any reason, do they confer any bonuses onto the unit?

## Ruling

No, not unless they have a special rule stating otherwise. Characters must lead from the front or retire to the rear.
