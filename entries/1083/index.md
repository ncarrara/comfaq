## Question

Does a unit that includes a Grail Monk with a Blessed Triptych lose Stubborn if the Grail Monk is killed before the rule can be used?

## Ruling

No. As per the errata, a unit that includes a Blessed Triptych becomes Stubborn, regardless of whether or not the model that purchased that upgrade has been killed or not.
