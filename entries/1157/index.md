## Question

What happens if a unit in Lance formation that declares a charge finds its movement blocked by the presence of an enemy unit to its flank, where the unit gets wider?

## Ruling

Such situations are covered by the rules for Accidental Contact During a Charge. If the Lance can wheel around the enemy unit, it should do so. If not, the Lance has two choices; halting its charge or continuing ahead and making contact with the enemy unit, treating it as a redirected charge.
