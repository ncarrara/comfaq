## Question

Against which models do the various instances of Hatred (X) apply?

## Ruling

Some instances of Hatred are keyword-based, applying against models identified by a given keyword appearing on their Unit Card or in one of their Special Rules. These are: enemy Characters (Troop Type), enemy Wizards (typically identified in the Magic section of the Unit Card), Undead models (models with either the Nehekharan Undead or Necromantic Undead special rule), Daemonic models (models with the Daemonic special rule), and Daemons of Slaanesh/Tzeentch/Nurgle/Khorne (models with the corresponding Daemon of... special rule).

Other instances are faction-based, applying against all models belonging to a given faction. In several cases, the rules use legacy Warhammer Fantasy Battles faction names as shorthand (or as an unchecked carryover): Orcs & Goblins (Orc & Goblin Tribes), Dwarfs (Dwarfen Mountain Holds), High Elves (High Elf Realms), Empire (Empire of Man), Beasts of Chaos (Beastmen Brayherds).

Units taken as Mercenaries or Allies belong only to their own faction (if any) for the purposes of Hatred. By contrast, if a unit is fielded as part of another faction's Army Composition List (excluding Mercenaries and Allies), it is considered to belong to both its own faction and the host faction for the purposes of Hatred. For example, Black Orcs fielded in a Chorfs army belong to both O&G and Chorfs; a Chaos Giant fielded in a WoC army belongs to both BB and WoC; a Chaos Spawn fielded in a BB army belongs to both WoC and BB.

Note that, unlike Hatred (X), Fear of Elves is a race-based rule. The rule explicitly states that Elves “of any type” cause Fear, making clear that it applies based on a model's race rather than a keyword or faction classification.
