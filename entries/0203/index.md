## Question

How many demonic gifts can a demon of chaos character buy? Does a character equipped with a Daemonic Locus count as a Battle Standard Bearer?

## Ruling

Since there are no categories for the demonic gifts mentioned as in the rulebook for magic items, a character can buy as many different gifts as his point limit allows. Demonic gifts without an asterik can only be taken once per army. The character with Daemonic Locus is a BSB in all respects, in addition to having the Daemonic Locus special rule and for characters, only a demonic locus can buy a single icon.
