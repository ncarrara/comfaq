## Question

When are Ambushers placed into reserve and when is this declared?

## Ruling

If your army includes any Ambushers that you wish to hold in reserve, set those units aside before deployment begins, making it clear to your opponent that they are Ambushers and will be held in reserve, rather than be deployed with the rest of your army.
