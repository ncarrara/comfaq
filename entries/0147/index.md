## Question

Can a mounted Character on light chariot (that has to adapt open order formation from its mount) joins a unit that has adopted close order formation?

## Ruling

Yes, the rule for mounted character formation ceases to apply as soon as he joins the unit and ceases to be an independent unit - the character will adopt the units formation.
