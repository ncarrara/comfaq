## Question

Does a unit count as being obscured when some of the models within it are behind others?

## Ruling

No. A unit cannot be obscured from the enemy by itself. What’s more, models within a unit do not obscure the enemy from other models within the same unit. They either completely block line of sight, or present no obstruction.
