## Question

When do units actually conduct Overrun?

## Ruling

Overrun is a pursuit move. The unit has the choice to attempt to restrain or reform, same as for Pursuit or to Overrun and makes then a normal pursuit move bust moves directly forwards. So, the unit moves after break tests.
