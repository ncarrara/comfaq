## Question

For the purposes of other upgrades, what types of armour are the Armour of Silvered Steel and the Armour of Meteoric Iron?

## Ruling

If a suit of magic armour gives an armour value of 6+, it should be considered light armour. If it gives an armour value of 5+, it should be considered heavy armour, and if it gives an armour value of 4+ or better, it should be considered full plate armour.
