## Question

Q: The GW FAQ says that an effect which can re-roll a failed casting roll does not work on a miscast. Does this ruling also apply to effects that allow you to re-roll any casting roll, such as Tome of Spellcraft?

## Ruling

Yes, the ruling states that a miscast cannot be re-rolled and this applies regardless of the type of re-roll ability.
