## Question

When a unit redresses the ranks, must the width of its front rank be increased or decreased as equally as possible on either side?

## Ruling

Yes. The exception to this is when the width of the front rank is increased or decreased by an odd number of models. In such cases, one side of the unit will gain or lose one more model than the other side.
