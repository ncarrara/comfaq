## Question

When dispelling Remains in Play spells during the Conjuration sub-phase, can a dispel attempt be made multiple times (by multiple wizards, for example) against the same spell?

## Ruling

No. You may attempt to dispel each Remains in Play spell a single time per Conjuration sub-phase, choosing either a wizard within wizardly dispel range or by making a fated dispel. The same wizard may be chosen to use their wizardly dispel this way against any number of Remains in Play spells.
