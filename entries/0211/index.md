## Question

Some Options (Equipment) confer Special Rules to Characters. Do Mounts also benefit from these special rules if they affect the Armour Save? Can this special rule be used to overcome a max. armour save?

## Ruling

Yes, these special rules affect also the mount. No, the max. Armour Save (3+) for Monsters, Chariots and War Machines is unaffected by this and will cap the Armour Save.
