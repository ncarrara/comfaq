## Question

How do abilities like Gnashing Maws (gigantic spawn of chaos) interact with regeneration? What about effects that trigger when a model loses its last wound, such as Timmm-berr!, Grail Pendant or Explosive Demise (warpfire dragon)?

## Ruling

Regeneration is no longer a save, so a wound from Gnashing Maws counts as an unsaved wound regardless of whether regeneration is successful. Effects that require a model to lose its last wound will only trigger if the model actually loses its last wound after any regeneration saves attempted.
