## Question

Can Skirmishers change their facing during a move? Can they change direction? How do they charge? And how does forming up work with Skirmishers?

## Ruling

Normal Movement and Marching
Skirmishers may freely change direction and pivot during normal movement and marching.

Charging with Skirmishers
During a Charge, they follow the charge criteria, therefore may change direction only once and must follow the shortest path to the closest visible model in the target unit.

The closest model in the charging unit moves first:
•    A single pivot must be made as early as possible in order to contact the nearest model in the charged unit via the shortest route.
•    Move straight, with your front facing directly forward, into base contact.

Charging Skirmishers with Skirmishers
The first moving model forms the center of the fighting rank upon contact. Other charging models have to form up (see below) in even numbers to the left and the right of that model. Maximum one model may be added in excess to that unevenly to one side. Against skirmishers you maximize only against the first contacted model of the enemy unit; adjust the path of the first charging model if it brings more of your models into base contact with the first contacted model. You do not have to alter your charge path in order to maximize the number of models in the enemy unit's fighting rank.
Skirmishers Charging formed units
When charging formed units, each model charges individually to form up (see below) to the charge target and form the fighting rank, so they can actively maximize base contact. Therefore, you have to prioritize base contact over the shortest route.

Units in Formed Formations (Open Order, Close Order etc.) charging Skirmishers
Any unit charging a unit of Skirmishers must take the shortest route to contact the closest visible model. The Skirmishers then form up (see below).

Forming Up (as charger against the enemy unit, building the fighting rank or if charged)
During Forming up, Skirmish models move in an order of their controlling player's choosing. They may move through friendly models and may pivot (once if charging). They form up edge to edge, front corner to front corner against the enemy fighting rank or behind if they have not sufficient movement. No model may move further than their M value, or their M value + the charge roll when charging. All Models unable to fully reach their required position in the ranks are removed as casualties (if the first model contacted is removed, others can still try to form up).
