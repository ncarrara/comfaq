## Question

Can you see a unit that is only partially on a hill and can you see a model that is only partially on a hill?

## Ruling

Yes, given no other unit obstructs that LoS. Treat models and units partially on a hill as they were on the ground, but the hill itself blocks no line of sight to those models that are partially on the hill.
