## Question

How is cover determined?

## Ruling

Cover is a measure of how obscured LoS is. How much of the "other model or unit" is visible or obscured is determined considering the definition of LoS, including rules that define when models do or don't block Line of Sight (Large Target, Insignificant, Vantage Point). This means, among others, that non-Large models do not provide cover if the shooting or target unit is a Large Target, that models not on a Vantage Point (Hill) do not provide cover if the shooting or target unit is entirely on a Vantage Point (Hill), and that Insignificant models never provide cover. Remember that Large Targets never benefit from cover. Note that for terrain categories that do not have a specific rule for Line of Sight (e.g. buildings, linear obstacles, ruins, battlefield decoration…), Line of Sight has to be determined by checking the actual "true" Line of Sight from the model, if not stated otherwise by the tournament organizer.
