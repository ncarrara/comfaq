## Question

How do Skirmishers conduct a flee move or Give Ground? What happens when they flee through units?

## Ruling

If in Skirmish Formation (not formed up), determine center-to-center, then flee  with all models parallel in this direction without pivoting the unit. Give Ground the same way, stopping the whole unit if at least one model encounters an obstacle.

If any model in the skirmish unit moves through another unit, then all models continue moving in the same direction until they are no longer within 1" of an enemy or overlapping a friendly unit, taking peril tests as normal.

If models flee through units, move the whole unit the same distance.  If the unit encounters an impassable terrain piece, each model moves as close as possible along that terrain piece and returns then to the original flee path

During Flee or Give Ground, if at least one model moves off the board, the whole unit is destroyed.
