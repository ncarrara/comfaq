## Question

How do you defend an Low linear obstacle? Aka how do you move up to it to defend it?

## Ruling

You have to wheel forward, until your front rank is parallel aligned with the LLO. You do not count as defending the low linear obstacle if your front rank exceeds the LLO at any point or if your front rank is not entirely aligned to the LLO, be it due to other models or insuffcient movement .
