## Question

Does a wizarding hat allow a character to buy arcane items? If the Character was a Wizard before and buys an arcane item. Does the Arcane Item affect spellcasting and spell generation of this model?

## Ruling

No, see GW FAQ. As explained in the GW FAQ, the wearer is not actually a Wizard, but instead the Hat is the wizard. Since the hat is not affected by armour the bearer wears, it is also not affected by arcane items.
