## Question

What are the size restrictions for player-created terrain, such as the forest from Woodland Ambush or the Herdstone? Can these be asymmetrical, hollow, or other unusual shapes?

## Ruling

For player-created forests, they must be between 3" at their narrowest and 9" at their widest point, which limits most unusual shapes. The Herdstone must be no more than 6" at its widest point, but has no minimum size or limits on unusual shapes. However, while there is no specific limitation in the rules, it is recommend that players check with the TO of their event before entering a tournament with a terrain piece that has a very unusual shape (especially if the shape is designed to provide a specific competitive advantage, such as a hollow herdstone) as it is common for TOs to disallow the use of these irregular designs.
