## Question

Can you take a virtue of the duty or ideal even if that means that your general has not the highest Ld in your army?

## Ruling

Yes, only the BSB is mentioned in GWs FAQ on the highest leadership. This also means in your army you can include mercenaries or other characters that are not able to be the general with a higher leadership than your general.
