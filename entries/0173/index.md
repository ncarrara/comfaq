## Question

For monster handlers, is the D6 roll to check whether a wound removes a handler or makes the monster lose a wound only during combat, or for any unsaved wounds?

## Ruling

The roll occurs for any unsaved wounds, since the last phrase of the paragraph is also not related only to combat. The "in combat" only applies to the first phrase.
