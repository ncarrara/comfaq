## Question

When do you actually conduct accidental contact during a charge? Do you get a charge reaction against it? What happens if you have Fly? And what happens if the original charge target fled?

## Ruling

If wheeling around the obstacle would cause you to do a failed charge, or if the unit is not able to wheel around the obstacle unit at all, you conduct accidental contact during a charge. The charging unit only counts as having redirected prior so it can make contact - you don't get a charge reaction, which only is available after a unit is selected as charge target during a redirect or charge declaration.

A charging unit must endeavour to bring as many models as possible within its front rank into base contact with models in the charged unit. That is the highest priority and the unit must use Fly (X) if needed. You determine if Fly (X) is needed when the unit starts its charge move. If the charging unit with Fly(X)  would make a failed charge and ends on top of the enemy unit, do accidental contact. However, if you fly over/behind the enemy unit, you cannot do accidental contact as it is not the enemy unit, but the missing charge distance that leads to the failed charge.
If the charge target declared flee or is fleeing for any reason, you do not conduct accidental contact during a charge at all, as you have to check for accidental contact during Running Down the Foe. During Running down the Foe, you charge as normally and you never make a failed charge, thus you always try to wheel or move around the obstacle unit. Only if you cannot do that, you will make accidental contact. This means, in most caes, that units with Fly (X) or Skirmishers go over or around obstacle units.
