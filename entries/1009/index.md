## Question

In a doubles event, as described in the Matched Play Guide, which armies, if any, can a Renegade Crowns army ally with?

## Ruling

If the primary force is a Renegade Crowns, Empire of Man, or Kingdom of Bretonnia army, Renegade Crowns can be fielded as trusted allies.
