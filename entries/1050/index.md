## Question

When shooting a cannon, a line of sight is required to the target point, but what about the strike point?

## Ruling

A cannon does not require a line of sight to the strike point. However, if anything that would stop a cannonball from bouncing (see 5. Crunch, on page 227) lies between the target point and the strike point, the cannonball will hit it and stop. In other words, you cannot use a high result on the Artillery dice to 'glitch' a cannonball through an impregnable obstacle.
