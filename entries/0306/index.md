## Question

Ethereal treats all terrain as open ground, and Grinding Wheels does the same for Low Lineae Obstacles. How does this interact with units that are defending a LLO?

## Ruling

Because the charge is a move, these units fully ignore the LLO and will move on top of it. For all purposes, units with these rules ignore defended LLO rules.
