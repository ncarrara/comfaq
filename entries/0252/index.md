## Question

Multiple Charges into Skirmishers: What happens if you can go straight forward in but also you can maximize if you go into another side of the unit?

## Ruling

Charge to the side, maximizing has priority, but remember that you are only allowed to do one wheel, sometimes you will be able to do this, while sometimes you have to priorize charging over maximizing - use the principles of charging, priorizing top to bottom.
