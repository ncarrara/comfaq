## Question

Do Characters and Crews benefit from the special rules of its mount and vice versa? If a Character uses an item, spell or ability that specifically affects the caster, does the caster's mount (including chariot crew etc.) benefit from that spell? Can champions use these items?

## Ruling

Unless noted otherwise, the split profile mechanics make any special rule that applies to one element (chariot, character, crew or beasts) apply to the others as well.
Special Rules are shared- both positive and negative.

The same is true for all effects that affect the "model" or "unit", such as Daemonic Vessel.

However, if a spell, item or ability that is not a special rule specifies the caster or character (wearer or bearer) (such as Mantle of Ghorok, or Potion of Fervour) gaining an effect will only affect the character (or champion) itself and not their mount or any components of that split profile model.
