## Question

If a unit of Scouts is deployed completely within my deployment zone, can they be deployed within 12" of the enemy, and can they charge in the first turn?

## Ruling

No. Regardless of where they are deployed, a unit deployed as Scouts must abide by the rules for deploying as Scouts.
