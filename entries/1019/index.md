## Question

Can a character replace the armour with which they come equipped with a lighter, magical armour in order to bypass restrictions imposed on other upgrades?

## Ruling

A character can replace one type of armour with another. However, for the purposes of any available upgrades that are restricted based on the type of armour worn, the character is still considered to be wearing the armour they come equipped with.
