## Question

Master Rune of Hesitation says the enemy unit "does not count as having charged for the purpose of choosing which weapons to use or using any special rules it may have." How does this affect weapons that can be used whether charging or not charging, such as cavalry spears or flails?

## Ruling

Because the rune only says "for the purpose of choosing," this means that the charging unit cannot choose to use lances. However, it does not negate any benefits those weapons gain when charging (except if they gain special rules, which the rune negates as normal). For example, a flail would grant +2S for charging, but not Armourbane (1).
