## Question

Motley Crew: When do you determine which casualties are removed in combat / what the majority of models is?

## Ruling

Check at the beginning of each initiative step when attacks are made. Check the majority of models of both fighting ranks, first and second, for the majority of models and use their armour save. Then for all unsaved wounds, remove casualties among those which have a majority. Apply the principles of casualty removal (first rank eligible to fight first, then second, then support attacks, then stepping forward) only among the models who have a majority. This can lead to the case that models from the second fighting rank are removed before models in the first fighting rank. Models that do not belong the the majority of the fighting rank are only removed after all models of the unit that do belong to the majority of the fighting rank for each initiative step. Note that a champion that choses a wholly different weapon counts as a separate pool. In case of a tie, the controlling player decides which armour save to use and which models to remove (you have to remove the models of which you use the armour save).
