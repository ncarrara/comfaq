## Question

Whilst running down the foe, is a charging unit considered to be pursuing a fleeing enemy for the purposes of crossing the edge of the battlefield, etc.?

## Ruling

Yes, a unit that runs down the foe is very much pursuing a fleeing enemy whilst doing so.
