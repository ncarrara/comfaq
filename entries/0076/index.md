## Question

Can the rules Shield of the Lady and Verminous Valour, which both allow a character to ‘retire’ by ‘moving through the ranks’, be used outside of combat? These rules specify they can be used 'at any time', but also directly refer to 'the fighting rank' and 'the combat', and the rule for 'moving through the ranks’.

When must retired characters (from Refusing a Challenge, Too Many Characters, Shield of the Lady or Verminous Valour) "un-retire", a.k.a. return to their original (front rank) position?

What happens when a unit falls below Unit Strength 10 after a Handmaiden retires to the rear rank of that unit?

## Ruling

These rules directly refer to 'the fighting rank' and 'the combat' which only apply in combat, and involve 'moving through the ranks' which requires the unit to be engaged and the character to return to its original position as soon as it disengages: Unretire when the unit is not engaged in the current combat anymore, provided that that combat does not continue due to a Follow Up or Pursuit move (GW FAQ confirmed that an ongoing challenge continues in that case).

Note that you cannot use it as a reaction on already stated or announced actions. This means while resolving a combat you have to use it at the end of an initiative step, before targets of models' attacks are announced. You can use it outside of resolving the combat at any time, given the unit is engaged in combat.

Note that the Unit Strength 10 condition for shield of the Lady is checked at the moment the Handmaiden retires to the rear rank. If the condition is met at that time, the Lady’s effects remain active until the combat ends, even if the unit later falls below Unit Strength 10.
