## Question

Do fanatics now die when moving through a unit that is entrenched or under the effect of Earthen Ramparts?

## Ruling

If the unit is under the effect of Earthen Ramparts but wasn’t charged, they hit the unit since earthen ramparts specifies it only works if charged. Against entrenched or earthen ramparts when charged, the fanatics hit the obstacle and die.
