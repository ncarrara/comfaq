## Question

If a Wizard has joined a unit, can they cast spells from any rank, or must they be in the front rank?

## Ruling

Unless they have a special rule that states otherwise, a Wizard must be in the front rank (or the first fighting rank) of their unit to cast spells. Like all characters, Wizards bring no benefit to their unit by skulking in the rear ranks.
