## Question

Does a unit of Battle Pilgrims that includes a Grail Reliquae benefit from Blessings of the Lady, or just the Grail Reliquae itself?

## Ruling

If the unit includes a Grail Reliquae, the entire unit gains Blessings of the Lady.
