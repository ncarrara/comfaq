## Question

How do multiple instances of Multiple Wounds special rule stack, e.g. Multiple Wounds (D3) and Multiple Wounds (2)?

## Ruling

It would be D3 +1 as MW (2) is effectively Wounds (+1)
Poll 04-2026:
Q: Are multiple instances of the Multiple Wounds (X) special rule cumulative?
A: No: take the highest. The MW rule is not a value modifier but instead a redefinition of what an unsaved wound does.
B: Yes: as it states on page 165 ‚Cumulative Special Rules‘: "the effect of the special rule in some way alters a characteristic, dice roll or any other value". In this case the end result (the effect) of MW (a special rule) alters a characteristic (the wound characteristic).
In addition, unlike magic resistance or frenzy, there is no wording or faq that ‚notes otherwise‘. Hence MW(X) + MW(Y) = MW(X+Y).
