## Question

Does a frenzied unit need to declare a charge if there is a friendly unit in it's way, if the friendly unit could itself declare a charge and move out of the way?

## Ruling

No, the frenzied unit only has to declare a charge if the blocking unit is declaring a charge but the blocking unit is not obliged to do so
