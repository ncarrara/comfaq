## Question

How are multiple Hits with the Multiple Wounds (X) special rule resolved against a unit with multiple Multiple Wound models? How do situations work where hits are inflicted on certain models (Cannons etc.)

## Ruling

Resolve attacks against the models that are hit in specific cases like cannons, or allocated attacks against champions or against the unit if the unit is attacked. This means, use the  weapon skill, toughness, armour and ward saves accordingly.

Unsaved wounds are then applied one after another to the unit, except in the case of champions being hit, which have their own conditions under which they are hit separately, including cannons (which can hit the champion separately). Page 102 and GW FAQ tell us to start with wounds that inflict Killing Blow, followed by Multiple Wound (X) wounds, and finally ending with single wound attacks. For wounds on the unit, Allocate these wounds starting with the model that is already wounded (ignore the champion if he is wounded already).
