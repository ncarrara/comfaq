## Question

Can a unit with the Finest Warhorses special rule re-roll the dice when using Swiftstride?

## Ruling

No. Finest Warhorses allows a unit to re-roll its Charge roll, but the D6 rolled when using Swiftstride is not part of the charge roll; it is rolled to give a modifier to the result of the Charge roll.
