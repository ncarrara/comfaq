## Question

Some spells and abilities ask a model to take an Initiative test, such as Terrors Below or Durthu's Wrath. How do temporary modifiers, such as spells, charging, or weapon special rules, affect these? Similarly, what initiative value is used when making an attack with a spell or weapon other than the one chosen to fight with during step 1.1 of combat?

## Ruling

A model's initiative for attacking, casting an assailment, or making a test always uses its current (modified) value. If tests are made in the Combat Phase, this includes the Charging Units Initiative bonus, even if the test is made out of combat in a turn that unit charges or counts as having charged. However, special rules from / on weapons like Strikes Last or Always Strikes First apply only to attacks made with those weapons - ignore these rules for all other purposes, including attacks made with different weapons or by different models on a combined profile. In the case of Spectral Doppelganger, attacks with a weapon with Strike First are still resolved on the same initiative as the spell is cast.
