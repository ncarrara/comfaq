## Question

How do you deteremine cover if a model can see multiple arcs of an opponent unit?

## Ruling

Check all line of sights from the shooting model to all models of the target unit, ignoring the lines which cross over models of the target unit. Models of the target unit you cannot see because of other models of that unit are ignored. Apply then the rules for cover, depending on how much of the unit is obscured. Note that according to GW FAQ, Woods give cover even without LOS crossing over them, as long as more than half of the target unit is within the Wood.
