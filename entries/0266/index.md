## Question

Does a champion count for the bretonnia exiles blessing if slain in a challenge - or can a champion even get a blessing of the lady if he kills a character or champion in a challenge?

## Ruling

No, only non-champion characters are counting for this and a champion cannot get the blessing separately from the unit
