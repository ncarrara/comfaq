## Question

Stupidity: What happens if a stupid unit does fbigo or rallies, can it reform upon a rally? What happens if the unit gives ground?

## Ruling

The unit does not give ground and cannot reform when rallying, changing face or use similar abilities (drilled, lumbering etc.). You cannot you use infernal gateway or travel mystical pathway on these units, a move is a move (except obligatory moves, which can be done)
