## Question

Do the modifiers for several Mortis Engine stack? How often do you roll on the Miscast table when you are in the Commanding Range of 2 Mortis Engines?

## Ruling

Given that the rules say you get the bonus if you are in range of "this model" the modifiers do stack for several Mortis Engines. In case ogf a Miscast an absolute number of rolls is given. So you roll twice no matter how many Mortis Engines are in range.
