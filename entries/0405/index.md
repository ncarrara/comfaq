## Question

An ogre loader for a Cathay cannon "joins the crew." What exactly does this mean? How do the gunpowder bombs work?

## Ruling

The ogre loader is considered part of the crew, and thus cannot be hit by enemy shooting (which all goes against the war machine itself). It confers its leadership to the War Machine, aswell as his special rules. The Ogre Loader is not placed on the table as crew, and will just function as other crew members of War Machines.
 In combat, the opponent has to chose wether to attack the Ogre Loader or the Crew as they form two different wound pools with different toughness. For malfunctions, the controlling player can chose which pools loses a wound. Only if both are reduced to 0 wounds, the war machine is destroyed.

When the crew makes ranged attacks using gunpowder bombs, make 4 attacks: one for the ogre, and one for each crew member
