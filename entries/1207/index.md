## Question

What happens if a Scroll of Transmogrification is used on a Bound spell?

## Ruling

After testing against the item's Power Level (in place of its Level of Wizardry), it may be turned into an inanimate frog, rendering it useless until it turns back. During each Start of Turn sub-phase, you may roll a D6 for each transmogrified item in your army to see if it returns to normal, as described on page 343.
