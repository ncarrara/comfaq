## Question

Is a character that joins another unit still considered a unit for any rules purpose? What about special rules that unit has, but the character hasn't or vice versa? What happens if a character marches when joining a unit or if the chracter joins a unit that has marched? How do spells interact with characters and units? How do you leave units?

## Ruling

Characters & Units
They form together a single unit, counting together for the Unit Strength of the unit. A Character cannot be targeted inside of a unit with an ability or spell that targets a unit, but by those which target a model or character. If all RnF-models are removed as casualties, the unit ceases to exist immediately. Any special rules of the unit cease to exist (characters still have their own special rules). If a spell was only on the unit (not being cast by self range by one of the characters), the characters lose the effect of that spell. All characters joined to the unit must potentially, depending on Unit Strength and phase, do break checks and panic tests for "nearby friend destroyed", but will only do fall back in good order if failing panic.

Movement
If a character moved or marched before joining a unit, the unit will not count as moved/marched and vice versa.
The character will lose any rules granted by the unit the moment he leaves the unit or the unit ceases to exist (see below).
The Movement Characteristic is checked when a character or unit begins their movement. Any spells or effects (positive or negative) affect the character's movement during the Remaining Moves phase in which the character leaves the unit. This means that if the unit is affected by Miasmic Mirage, the character cannot march, and if the unit is affected by Steed of Shadows, the character may move using Fly (12). If for any reason the character does not follow the Lone Character rules when not in a unit (such as a character on a light chariot), then they must adhere to the normal movement rules for their respective unit type when leaving the unit. If the character becomes a lone character upon leaving, they move as a skirmisher, and move through other models in their unit freely when leaving.

Spells
If a character under an effect "that affects the caster and any unit he has joined" leaves a unit, only the caster has the effect, but if he joins another unit, that unit will benefit from that effect too. Note that spells that affect a character will affect the joined unit too, as described on pg. 207. (Note the GW FAQ about creating illegal situations).

The Unit's Rules
Whenever the word 'unit' is mentioned in a rules interaction (Spell, Special rule, etc.) that affects the unit the character has joined, apply rules for both the character and the unit he joined. This means that while a character does not receive the special rules of the unit he joins, he gains special rules and effects that are granted by the unit's special rules or effects the unit is under, which state that the unit is affected.
Please note that some special rules could cease to apply if a character without a special rule joins a unit or vice versa (e.g. Counter Charge).
Special rules that only need the majority of models to have the special rule will work (e.g. Reserve Move).

The Character's Rules
Special Rules a character has only apply to the unit if "and any unit they have joined" is mentioned or similar.
Special Rules the character has that mention "a unit with this special rule" will still affect him and he is able to use them, even if he has joined a unit (e.g. Veteran, First Charge), unless it is stated that the special rule will translate to the unit (Stupidity). Else, they will not affect the unit he has joined.
