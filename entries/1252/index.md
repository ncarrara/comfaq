## Question

If a model has Killing Blow or Monster Slayer, does a roll To Wound of a natural 6 cause an enemy to lose all of its remaining Wounds even if that enemy would normally be too tough to wound?

## Ruling

No. If a model cannot wound an enemy, it cannot kill it.
