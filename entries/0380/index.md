## Question

If a Character is placed in the rear rank outside of combat because there is no room for them in the front rank, do they count as retired? What happens if there is also no space in the rear rank or he shrinks back into the fighting/front rank? What happens if there is space again for characters that retired because of too many characters or there are only places in the fighting rank left due to shrinking units?

## Ruling

A Character that is not leading from the front is considered retired and cannot confer Inspiring Presence, Hold Your Ground, Aura abilities or similar static benefits unless a rule explicitly states otherwise. Any benefits from spells with the range 'Self' are lost, but spells and abilities that targeted other units or are resolved for global effects remain in effect. Retired models will be counted towards the Units Unit Strength and towards the formation to determine rank bonus, combat order and marching column. They are still considered part of the unit for any check on unit type, model special rule affiliation (unit 'consists entirely' etc.) and target legality (ethereal, unbreakable etc.)

If the character cannot be placed in the rear rank, place him in the closest spot from the rear that is away from a fighting or front rank to retire him. Adjacent characters can be placed behind the rear rank if they retire.

If the unit shrinks down and the character returns by this to the front or fighting rank only because no other position away from a fighting rank is available, he unretires after all casualties are removed, after the initiative step and after any panic checks are resolved, but if this happens in combat he counts as having stepped forward. Outside of combat the usual rules for unretiring characters take place and characters return to the front rank if the formation allows to.
