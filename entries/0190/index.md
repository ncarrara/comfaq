## Question

Can a Champion that is not on the table (because he is in Ambush, or hold back as Reserves) Challenge or accept the Challenge of a Warrior Duel ?

## Ruling

Yes, nothing in the wording of the rule implies that the Champion's Unit of the Marauder or their opponent have to be actually on the battlefield to fight in the Warrior Duel.
