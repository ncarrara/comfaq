## Question

Who can allocate attacks to a single model in a Lance Formation?

## Ruling

Models that are physically in base contact with a character or champion may allocate their attacks to that model (usually the tip of the Lance and the models it physically makes contact with). Models that are not physically in base contact with the Lance are considered to be in base contact with the Lance unit, and vice versa, but not with any singular model in particular.
