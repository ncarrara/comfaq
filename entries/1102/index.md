## Question

Models in a fighting rank cannot fight if they are killed before they have a chance to fight. But can models in a second fighting rank (in the case of units within the Press of Battle rule) fight if the model in front is slain, and can a model make a supporting attack if the model(s) in the fighting ranks(s) in front of it are slain?

## Ruling

Yes. Unless they have been killed, models that are in a fighting rank or that are able to make supporting attacks can do so as normal. In other words, casualties inflicted reduce, firstly, the number of models in the fighting rank(s) that are able to fight (starting with the first fighting rank) and, secondly, the number of models in the 'supporting rank' that are able to fight.
