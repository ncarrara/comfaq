## Question

If a unit defending a Low Linear Obstacle, or counting as defending a Low Linear Obstacle (Dig In, Earthen Ramparts), is charged by an enemy unit that is unable to align, does the defending unit have to align against the enemy unit, hence losing its status?

## Ruling

Only for Dug In or Earthen Ramparts, as there is no physical LLO that prevents aligning. With a physical Low Linear Obstacle, the units cannot align to each other, as no base contact to align on was established.
