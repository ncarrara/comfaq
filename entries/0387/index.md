## Question

Can the Battle Standard be "claimed as a trophy" in the same way as any other standard (as described p.200), by killing the BSB in combat or running him down?

## Ruling

Yes, and the Victory Points obtained are cumulative to the Victory Points granted for slaying the BSB.
