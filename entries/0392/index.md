## Question

When a unit fleeing from a charge or a combat flees off the battlefield or is destroyed during its flee move (e.g. due to peril tests, dangerous terrain, fanatics, vortexes,...), what happens to a charging/pursuing unit?

## Ruling

Complete the fleeing unit’s movement, and leave it in place. Then complete charges and pursuits, as if that unit was still fully on the board.

If the fleeing unit is destroyed during the flee move, conduct pursuit and charges towards the position where the last model was removed.
