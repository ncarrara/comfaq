## Question

When a Tomb Scorpion or unit of Necroserpents makes use of the Terrors Below special rule, is the number of Initiative tests the enemy unit takes equal to its own Unit Strength, or equal to the Unit Strength of the unit using the special rule?

## Ruling

The latter. This special rule represents an emerging unit displacing earth and dragging infantry to their doom. How much earth the unit can displace is determined by its own Unit Strength, not by that of the enemy.
