## Question

Can a model that is only in base contact with a champion or a character allocate attacks to the unit itself or does it have to allocate all attacks to the champion or character?

## Ruling

Yes, the rules which force units to allocate attacks are only relevant for multiple units, but characters and the unit forme one single unit, you can allocate attacks to the unit.
