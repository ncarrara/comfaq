## Question

Do Excess Wounds from Multiple Wounds (X) count for Overkill vs. non-Character models (Champions)?

## Ruling

Yes. All Challenges, both vs. Characters and Champions allow Overkill, so excess wounds from multiple wounds should be counted against Champions as well. Champions are treated as Characters for this Purpose.
