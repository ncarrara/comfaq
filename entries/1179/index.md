## Question

If two units are engaged across a low linear obstacle, are they limited to making just a single attack because they aren't in base contact?

## Ruling

No. Whilst a charging unit will become disordered, and whilst Impact Hits and Stomp Attacks cannot be made due to the presence of the obstacle (as stated above), units are otherwise able to fight normally across a low linear obstacle, as if they were in base contact.
