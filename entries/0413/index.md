## Question

How does the Move Through Cover rule apply to units where some models have MTC and other models do not?

## Ruling

The -1M from difficult terrain affects the entire unit, even models not actually in that terrain, but models with the Move through cover special rule will not suffer the -1 Movement penalty.

If a character has joined the unit without Move through cover, he will suffer the penalty (the unit won't) and the unit moves at the modified speed of its slowest model.

In Regard to the higher or lower dice during charges, the unit will always take the lower die if any models do not have the Move Through Cover rule, even if the characters do not actually cross difficult terrain.
