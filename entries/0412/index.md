## Question

The rule for Transformation of the Dragon states that the new form cannot be placed within 1" of friendly models, within 3" and within 6" of its previous form. The new FAQ states that when the Dragon is in base-to-base contact with enemy models and transforms, it must remain in b2b. As it's impossible to satisfy all of these conditions, how should the rule be played? What happens if there is no room for the new model to be in b2b with the enemy unit?

## Ruling

When a Cathayan Dragon is in combat, their transformation rule ignores the requirements to be further than 1" from friendly models or 3" from enemy models and their base is allowed to overlap their previous form.
If there is no room to place the new model in b2b with the enemy unit due to allied models occupying their full front arc, simply nudge those friendly units aside by the minimum distance necessary to fit the Dragon's new form. However, if there is no space to fit the new Dragon model due to enemy units (even ignoring the 1" rule) or allied units not engaged in the combat, the dragon cannot transform.
