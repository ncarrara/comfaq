## Question

How does the 'Resurrecting the Fallen' ability work with units in Skirmish formation?

## Ruling

Just as GW FAQ states, you have to add th emodels in coherency. Since all skirmishers are resurrected at the same time, each model added has to be in coherency with another model of the unit that was already present before the resurrection.
