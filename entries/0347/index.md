## Question

When charging a unit in an arc that features an incomplete rank, how are you to make contact with that unit? What happens if you overrun or pursuit into such a unit, but only hit the template or rather footprint?

## Ruling

If charging, Use the footprint of the unit, extending the incomplete rank or file to be complete, and have the charging unit make contact (and maximize contact with) models who would be in those positions.
If Overruning or during a pursuit, you only make contact with such an unit if you hit actual models. In that case, you would conduct your move wheeling to maximise towards the footprint of the unit.
