## Question

The Fighting Rank. How do you determine the number of attacks of a model with closing in and stepping forward? How does the new FAQ, affect this? How are casualties removed in combat? How does this affect the number of attacks a unit can make?

## Ruling

Which models can attack and the number of attacks those models can make is determined in step 1.1. Per the combat phase sequence (as this step is not repeated) this is not revisited and largely does not change during a fight (this is also known as the "Combat Lock" or "Combat Freeze"). The FAQ about Press of Battle and Stepping forward from a fighting rank into another creates a clear exception to this norm: Models that were already in a fighting rank and determined to have been able to fight in step 1.1., can still make any remaining attacks they have after stepping up as they are closing in. In addition, if stepping up and closing in brought them into base to base contact with the enemy they may make their full number of attacks rather than just a singular attack  in order to  "maximizing their number of attacks".

For all other cases, the number of attacks is not changed when models Step Forward, move to replace characters that retire from the Fighting Rank, or change position in the unit due to shrinking units or other reasons.

Casualties are removed among models that can fight (within M") and from the ends of each rank (owner chooses from which end for every casualty):
- Firstly, from the first fighting rank,
- secondly, from the second fighting rank (if applicable),
- thirdly, from models making supporting attacks (if applicable),
- finally from models of the unit that cannot fight.
Remove actual models from the rear (mark the models counting as casualties). Once you removed your first casualty and if the back rank is incomplete, you must continue to remove models from the same direction in the rear rank.

Remember that a model that steps up into a different fighting rank due to casualty removal must allocate its attacks only against an enemy unit that it could have attacked prior to stepping up and models that step up into fighting ranks from outside cannot attack t all (as determined in 1.1)
