## Question

When using Domination as a secondary objective, do you get the Victory Points for having more than double the enemy's Unit Strength in a table quarter if there are no enemy models in that quarter?

## Ruling

No. To be more than double the enemy’s Unit Strength, the enemy has to have some models in the quarter. If they have none and you do, you gain the VP bonus for that achievement instead.
