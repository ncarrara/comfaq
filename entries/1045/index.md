## Question

Heavenly Beacon allows you to modify the roll by +1 or -1 when rolling to determine if Ambushers arrive. Is this cumulative? In other words, is it +2 or -2 if I have two Sky Lanterns?

## Ruling

No, it's not cumulative. You may apply a +1 or -1 modifier if you have one or more Sky Lanterns.
