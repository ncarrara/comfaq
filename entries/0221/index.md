## Question

Can a unit with Random movement reform when restraining from pursuing or following up, when rallying (including fall back in good order) or after destroying a fleeing enemy unit ?

## Ruling

Yes, the restriction on not being able to perform manoeuvres only applies when moving with their Random Movement characteristic.
