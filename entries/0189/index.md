## Question

What happens when a unit that gives ground cannot move more than 1" and will be within 1" of a unit it is in combat with, be it because of terrain or other units?

## Ruling

Since giving ground is not possible, the units remain locked in combat, but all units move as far as possible and remain locked at the new position (see GW FAQ).
