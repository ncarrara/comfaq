## Question

When using Evasive, how does it interact with Targeting, Magic Missiles, Bombardment, Cannon Fire and Line of Sight?

## Ruling

Evasive can be used after a  unit has been targeted by shooting. If the unit evades out of LOS or out of range, the attacks are lost. The following exceptions and special cases apply:
- Bombardment: You Target the unit by placing the template over it. If the unit uses Evasive, the template will not move with the unit, but will be resolved at the initial positon.
- Cannon Fire: If you target the enemy model, and the model moves by using evasive, the model and the target point are still separate entities. As you determine the target point in step 1, choose target point, which is the same as targeting, the point is then fixed on the initial position of the models base edge, similar to Bombardment - "once you have chosen your target point" puts marking the target point and targeting on the exact same step. The shot will always be conducted from the target point.
- Magic Missiles: Use evasive before the casting roll is even made, after the target is announced. The wizard then may chose to not make the casting roll. ("unit may continue with its shooting as declared")
