## Question

How is the distance of a wheel measured?

## Ruling

It is measured by taking a straight line from the outside front corner of the starting position to the outside front corner of the end position of the outermost model. For wheels that move over an angle greather than 90 degrees, measure the wheel in increments of (up to) 90 degrees.
