## Question

Some chariots are equipped with large scale missile weapons such as bolt throwers. Who shoots such weapons; the crew, the beasts that draw the chariot, the chariot itself, or a character mounted upon the chariot?

## Ruling

Missile weapons mounted on chariots (or howdahs) are shot by the crew, using their Ballistic Skill.
