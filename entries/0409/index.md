## Question

Are Fanatics units?

## Ruling

Yes, a fanatic is a unit (except for all cases that are explained and listed under their unit entry) as it has a model profile, including characteristics, special rules, equipment etc. This means they work as any other unit, with the following exceptions:
- cannot be charged or have attacks directed against them (with the exception of enemy shooting attacks, against which they count as lone characters) and cannot be targeted by any spells (with the exception of enemy magic missiles), not even by their owner.
- does not engage with other units, but follows the rules for fanatic movement when hitting them.
- do not give away victory points when dying.
- their special rule which allows units to move through them forces units to charge through fanatics towards their charge target.
Note that fanatics being units will force the following interactions:
- They count as unit for the 1" check of give ground, which is not being disabled by the Fanatic Movement rule.
- Fanatics can hit each other (see the COMFAQ entry for units destroyed while moving) and can never leave the board.
- They provide cover and will cause ambushers and units returning from offside the battlefield to keep distance to them.
- They will cause peril tests when units flee through them in addition to the Fanatic Ball & Chain rule.
- They will cause panic in units and will potentially cause units to flee from them if the fanatic is the closest enemy unit.
- Score domination.
