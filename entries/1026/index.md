## Question

Can a Bretonnian army pray for the Blessing of the Lady if it includes an allied contingent?

## Ruling

Yes, however an allied contingent of Bretonnians taken as part of any other army cannot pray. In order for Bretonnians to pray, the army they are part of must have the Blessings of the Lady rule, which only Kingdom of Bretonnia armies have.
